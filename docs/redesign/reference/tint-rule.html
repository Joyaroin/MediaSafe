Title tint

The only colour in the app besides danger. It comes from the poster, it is never painted as stored, and text over it has a floor it cannot fall below.

The rule

  1. 1
    Start from the stored poster colour title.poster_colour already exists. The nightly sweep and every add fill it in.
  2. 2
    Keep its hue A red poster gives a red band, a green poster a green one. The app never picks the colour.
  3. 3
    Set lightness to 52% A near-black poster and a pastel poster land at the same depth.
  4. 4
    Cap chroma at 0.17 A neon poster glows instead of shouting. A grey poster stays grey.
  5. 5
    No colour, no band A title with no stored colour shows the raised grey. Nothing is guessed.
In code One more constant, BAND, beside WASH and GLOW in app/poster-tint.ts. The band is the same in light and dark, so one value travels instead of two.

Worst case means pure white or pure black artwork under the scrim, in both themes, and every hue the band can take. The floors are 3:1 for large type and 4.5:1 for small.

Fixed on the canvas Tonight and Title now meet both floors. Before, the small “Up next” label sat at 56% scrim, which a bright episode still would have washed out.
