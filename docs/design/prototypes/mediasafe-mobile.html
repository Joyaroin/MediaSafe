Interaction model

Tabs: tap or drag the pager; underline slides 260ms. Watched toggles fill in place and never navigate; tapping the card opens details. Progress bars animate on the same write. Press feedback is a 1.5% scale. Sort is a navigation, filters and switches are local state. Every motion above is disabled under reduced-motion.
